Private by design. Conversion happens locally in your browser. Your drawing is never uploaded.
How it works: the drawing is traced in your browser. Inked pixels (alpha, or darkness on opaque images) are thinned to centre lines, split at crossings so each drawn line stays one stroke, simplified, and written as native Samsung Notes pen strokes (Pen tool) with the line's own colour and thickness. No image is embedded, so each stroke can be selected, moved and removed with the stroke eraser like strokes you drew yourself. Shading, gradients and texture are not reproduced: this is for line art.
