Private by design. Conversion happens locally in your browser. Your drawing is never uploaded.
How it works: the drawing is traced in your browser. Inked pixels (alpha, or darkness on opaque images) are thinned to centre lines, split at crossings so each drawn line stays one stroke, simplified, and written as native Samsung Notes strokes with the line's own colour and thickness: lines with the Pen, fading ends included, and shading with the Pencil, its transparency matching the tone (gradients in 10% steps). Small images are traced on an enlarged copy, so thin lines keep their place and width. No image is embedded, so each stroke can be selected, moved and removed with the stroke eraser like strokes you drew yourself.
